6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,610 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - wellbore instability: over-pressured shale sloughing.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,730 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  880 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,015 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,015 m MD.
