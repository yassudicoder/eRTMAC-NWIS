5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 07-04-2016
     While drilling Dihing at 140 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 3.9 m3/hr were measured.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.4 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 08-04-2016
     Hole washout logged at 300 m MD in Namsang; caliper 41.9 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.7 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 09-04-2016
     Bit and BHA balling up in the Girujan Clay at 750 m MD, SPP increase of 19.3 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.2 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 14-04-2016
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.35.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.9 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 12-04-2016
     Lost circulation encountered at 1,260 m MD in Tipam Sandstone. Loss rate recorded as 32.2 m3/hr.
     Pumped 82 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 27.6 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 18-04-2016
     Gas influx observed at 2,600 m MD in Barail Coal Shale; gas units rose to 1203. Pit gain 9.3 m3.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.26 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 57.5 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 21-04-2016
     Torque increased sharply at 2,850 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 18.3 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.1 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 27-04-2016
     Losses observed while cementing the 9-5/8in intermediate at 3,074 m MD; returns none.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 139.8 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 27-04-2016
     CBL indicated top of cement at 1,951 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate, 994 m below the planned top of 957 m.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 28.2 hrs.

[PAGE 4]

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 27-04-2016
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.217.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 27.7 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 02-05-2016
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.198.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 40.9 hrs.

5.12  Kopili Shale - 28-04-2016
     High torque and drag from 3,820 m MD in Kopili Shale; peak torque 37.0 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 58.3 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - kick: gas influx from coal seams.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,600 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,074 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,260 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,074 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,074 m MD.
