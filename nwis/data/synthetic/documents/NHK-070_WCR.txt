     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.5 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 12-04-2022
     Bit balling suspected at 920 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 49.6 ksc.

     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.0 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 17-04-2022
     Losses observed while cementing the 13-3/8in surface at 1,195 m MD; returns partial.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 52.6 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 17-04-2022
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.375.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.6 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 17-04-2022
     Primary cement job on the 13-3/8in surface failed acceptance; remedial squeeze carried out at 1,195 m MD.
     Squeeze held the pressure test; drilling resumed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 22.8 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 17-04-2022
     Top of cement logged at 481 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 28.2 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 17-04-2022
     Differential sticking at 1,920 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 40.8 t.
     Jarring continued for 21.9 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 27.3 hrs.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,310 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,195 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,195 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,195 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,920 m MD.
