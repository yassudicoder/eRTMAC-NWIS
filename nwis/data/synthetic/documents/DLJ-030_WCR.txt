5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 19-11-2020
     Hole washout logged at 150 m MD in Dihing; caliper 40.7 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.1 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 20-11-2020
     Mud loss of 4.3 m3/hr recorded between 560 m and 596 m MD in Namsang.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.9 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 23-11-2020
     Hole instability and sloughing from 1,020 m to 1,070 m MD in Girujan Clay.
     Mud weight raised to 1.18 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 41.9 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 03-12-2020
     CBL indicated top of cement at 180 m behind the 13-3/8in surface, 180 m below the planned top of 0 m.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 12.9 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 03-12-2020
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.05.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.7 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 03-12-2020
     Bond log showed channelling behind the 13-3/8in surface, casing poorly centralised.
     Additional centralisers run on the following string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.6 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 04-12-2020
     Differential sticking at 2,370 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 13.6 t.
     Jarring continued for 7.2 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.2 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 13-12-2020
     Differential sticking at 3,250 m MD across the Barail Coal Shale; maximum overpull 15.7 t.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 7.0 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.8 hrs.


[PAGE 4]
5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 27-12-2020
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.29.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.8 hrs.

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 27-12-2020
     Remedial cement squeeze performed behind the 9-5/8in intermediate at 3,284 m MD before drilling ahead.
     Squeeze held the pressure test; drilling resumed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 25.3 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 16-12-2020
     Partial mud losses of 11.0 m3/hr observed at 3,380 m MD (3,048 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous.
     Pumped 109 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.9 hrs.

5.12  Barail Arenaceous - 18-12-2020
     Influx taken at 3,680 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous. Well shut in, SIDPP 45.7 ksc; killed by drillers method.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.31 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 76.9 hrs.

5.13  Barail Arenaceous - 23-12-2020
     String became stuck at 3,850 m MD (3,459 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 60.7 t recorded.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 90.4 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 128.3 hrs.

5.14  Barail Arenaceous - 04-01-2021
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.268.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 54.7 hrs.

5.15  Kopili Shale - 30-12-2020
     Wellbore instability in Kopili Shale from 4,000 m MD. Excessive cavings (3.3 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.51 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.9 hrs.

5.16  Kopili Shale - 31-12-2020
     High torque and drag from 4,070 m MD in Kopili Shale; peak torque 17.4 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.1 hrs.

5.17  Sylhet Limestone - 05-01-2021
     Complete loss of circulation at 4,500 m MD on entering Sylhet Limestone.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 75.9 hrs.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,680 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,850 m MD.
- Sylhet Limestone - mud loss: total losses into fractured and karstified limestone.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,500 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,020 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,268 m MD.
