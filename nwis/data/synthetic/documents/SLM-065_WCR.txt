5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 18-08-2015
     While drilling Namsang at 180 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 2.9 m3/hr were measured.
     LCM pill of 159 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.3 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 19-08-2015
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Girujan Clay at 660 m MD; approximately 0.7 m3 recovered.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.22 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.2 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 20-08-2015
     Hole washout logged at 930 m MD in Tipam Sandstone; caliper 14.4 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.9 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 25-08-2015
     String became stuck at 1,680 m MD (1,680 m TVD) in Tipam Sandstone. Overpull of 45.1 t recorded.
     Jarring continued for 9.5 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 26.6 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 28-08-2015
     Partial mud losses of 9.4 m3/hr observed at 2,160 m MD (2,160 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     Pumped 119 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.9 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 01-09-2015
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 2,620 m MD; approximately 8.5 m3 recovered.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.23 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.8 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 07-09-2015
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 9-5/8in intermediate casing at 2,700 m MD.
     Job completed with reduced returns; top of cement confirmed by temperature survey.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 25.9 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 07-09-2015
     CBL indicated top of cement at 1,387 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate, 663 m below the planned top of 724 m.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 35.2 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 07-09-2015
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.324.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.6 hrs.

[PAGE 4]

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 06-09-2015
     Well kicked at 3,060 m MD (3,052 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Pit gain 5.0 m3, SIDPP 24.7 ksc. Well shut in.
     Weighted up to 1.32 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.7 hrs.

5.11  Kopili Shale - 12-09-2015
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.327.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.4 hrs.

5.12  Kopili Shale - 11-09-2015
     Gas influx observed at 3,620 m MD in Kopili Shale; gas units rose to 2025. Pit gain 4.9 m3.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.6 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 27.6 hrs.

5.13  Kopili Shale - 15-09-2015
     Torque increased sharply at 4,100 m MD while drilling Kopili Shale, peaking at 19.0 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.7 hrs.

5.14  Kopili Shale - 18-09-2015
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Kopili Shale at 4,340 m MD; approximately 10.1 m3 recovered.
     Mud weight raised to 1.56 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 46.0 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,680 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,620 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,700 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,700 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,060 m MD.
