5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 28-01-2014
     Hole washout logged at 120 m MD in Dihing; caliper 30.6 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.3 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 30-01-2014
     Hole instability and sloughing from 670 m to 700 m MD in Girujan Clay.

[PAGE 3]
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 41.1 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 02-02-2014
     Tight hole experienced at 1,000 m MD in Girujan Clay; 8 reaming passes required.
     Reamed and circulated the interval clean before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 33.8 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 04-02-2014
     Mud loss of 24.3 m3/hr recorded between 1,220 m and 1,228 m MD in Tipam Sandstone.
     Pumped 28 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.8 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 07-02-2014
     String became stuck at 1,460 m MD (1,460 m TVD) in Tipam Sandstone. Overpull of 48.2 t recorded.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 135 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 45.0 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 15-02-2014
     Pipe stuck at 2,540 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale. Free point established at 1844.3 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 49.2 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 56.1 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 18-02-2014
     Torque increased sharply at 2,650 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 31.9 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.6 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 01-03-2014
     Top of cement logged at 1,377 m against a planned top of 1,050 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.9 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 01-03-2014
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.1.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 53.3 hrs.

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 23-02-2014
     Gas influx observed at 3,150 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 2704. Pit gain 7.5 m3.
     Weighted up to 1.34 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 147.4 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 02-03-2014
     Mud loss of 11.3 m3/hr recorded between 3,240 m and 3,244 m MD in Barail Arenaceous.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,150 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  670 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,000 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,220 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,460 m MD.
