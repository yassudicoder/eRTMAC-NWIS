5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 30-01-2016
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 920 m MD. Excessive cavings (2.5 m3) over the shakers.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.6 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 31-01-2016
     Tight hole experienced at 1,180 m MD in Girujan Clay; 6 reaming passes required.

[PAGE 3]
     Reamed and circulated the interval clean before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 11.5 hrs.

5.3  Barail Coal Shale - 11-02-2016
     Well kicked at 2,830 m MD (2,830 m TVD) while drilling Barail Coal Shale. Pit gain 2.6 m3, SIDPP 20.3 ksc. Well shut in.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.27 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 42.3 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 14-02-2016
     High torque and drag from 2,960 m MD in Barail Coal Shale; peak torque 25.0 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.9 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 15-02-2016
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 3,030 m MD. Excessive cavings (8.4 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.25 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 39.6 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 19-02-2016
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.2.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 28.9 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 19-02-2016
     Remedial cement squeeze performed behind the 9-5/8in intermediate at 3,144 m MD before drilling ahead.
     Second squeeze required before an acceptable test was obtained.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 59.8 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - kick: gas influx from coal seams.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,830 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,030 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,144 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,144 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,180 m MD.
