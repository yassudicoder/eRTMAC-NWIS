5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 07-03-2012
     Hole washout logged at 110 m MD in Dihing; caliper 38.0 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.7 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 08-03-2012
     While drilling Namsang at 460 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 4.4 m3/hr were measured.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 2.7 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.4 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 10-03-2012
     Bit and BHA balling up in the Girujan Clay at 1,140 m MD, SPP increase of 19.2 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.4 hrs.

[PAGE 3]

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 12-03-2012
     Losses observed while cementing the 13-3/8in surface at 1,269 m MD; returns partial.
     Job completed with reduced returns; top of cement confirmed by temperature survey.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 30.2 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 12-03-2012
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.343.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.9 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 12-03-2012
     CBL indicated top of cement at 229 m behind the 13-3/8in surface, 229 m below the planned top of 0 m.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.2 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 13-03-2012
     Seepage losses (3.0 m3/hr) noted at 1,610 m MD in the Tipam Sandstone.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.2 hrs.

5.8  Tipam Sandstone - 14-03-2012
     Hole washout logged at 1,830 m MD in Tipam Sandstone; caliper 16.9 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.9 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,269 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,269 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,269 m MD.
- Dihing - hole washout: unconsolidated gravel eroded by high flow rate.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  110 m MD.
- Namsang - mud loss: seepage losses into coarse sand stringers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  460 m MD.
