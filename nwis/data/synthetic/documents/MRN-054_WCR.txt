5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 19-05-2011
     Hole washout logged at 270 m MD in Namsang; caliper 25.3 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.9 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 20-05-2011
     Bit and BHA balling up in the Girujan Clay at 560 m MD, SPP increase of 24.1 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.8 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 24-05-2011
     String became stuck at 1,040 m MD (1,039 m TVD) in Tipam Sandstone. Overpull of 34.9 t recorded.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 144 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 25.0 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 30-05-2011
     Lost circulation encountered at 2,130 m MD in Tipam Sandstone. Loss rate recorded as 10.6 m3/hr.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.3 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 01-06-2011
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,470 m MD. Excessive cavings (5.4 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.24 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.5 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 13-06-2011
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.113.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 56.2 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 05-06-2011
     Partial mud losses of 28.2 m3/hr observed at 2,970 m MD (2,565 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 23.1 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 33.4 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 09-06-2011
     Pipe stuck at 3,230 m MD while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Free point established at 1919.3 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 11.5 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 27.6 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 10-06-2011
     Influx taken at 3,270 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous. Well shut in, SIDPP 47.7 ksc; killed by drillers method.
     Weighted up to 1.31 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 44.8 hrs.

[PAGE 4]

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 20-06-2011
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 7in liner casing at 3,634 m MD.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 40.3 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 20-06-2011
     CBL indicated top of cement at 2,616 m behind the 7in liner, 152 m below the planned top of 2,464 m.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.1 hrs.

5.12  Kopili Shale - 17-06-2011
     Hole instability and sloughing from 3,770 m to 3,802 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Mud weight raised to 1.57 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 42.0 hrs.

5.13  Kopili Shale - 19-06-2011
     Gas influx observed at 3,800 m MD in Kopili Shale; gas units rose to 913. Pit gain 10.0 m3.
     Weighted up to 1.56 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 43.2 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,270 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,634 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,800 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,040 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,872 m MD.
