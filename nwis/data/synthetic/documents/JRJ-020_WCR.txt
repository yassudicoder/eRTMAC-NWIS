5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 19-02-2021
     Top of cement logged at 264 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.7 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 19-02-2021
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.306.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.3 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 02-03-2021
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.222.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 31.4 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 02-03-2021
     Primary cement job on the 9-5/8in intermediate failed acceptance; remedial squeeze carried out at 3,009 m MD.
     Second squeeze required before an acceptable test was obtained.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 55.8 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 06-03-2021
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.31.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.6 hrs.
