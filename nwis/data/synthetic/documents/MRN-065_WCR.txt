5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 22-12-2019
     Partial mud losses of 3.0 m3/hr observed at 10 m MD (10 m TVD) while drilling Dihing.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.3 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 23-12-2019
     Mud loss of 2.8 m3/hr recorded between 110 m and 152 m MD in Namsang.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 1.9 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.8 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 24-12-2019
     Bit and BHA balling up in the Girujan Clay at 840 m MD, SPP increase of 31.5 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.2 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 25-12-2019
     Seepage losses (3.3 m3/hr) noted at 900 m MD in the Tipam Sandstone.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 2.1 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.7 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 30-12-2019
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 13-3/8in surface casing at 900 m MD.
     Job completed with reduced returns; top of cement confirmed by temperature survey.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 45.0 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 30-12-2019
     Top of cement logged at 382 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 48.7 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 16-01-2020
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.228.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 23.6 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 06-01-2020
     Torque increased sharply at 2,920 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 33.6 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.0 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 09-01-2020
     Pipe stuck at 3,120 m MD while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Free point established at 2244.1 m.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 80 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 84.9 hrs.

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 14-01-2020
     Gas influx observed at 3,310 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 1897. Pit gain 10.1 m3.
     Weighted up to 1.35 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 131.4 hrs.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,120 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,310 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  900 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  900 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,880 m MD.
