5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 23-04-2025
     While drilling Dihing at 30 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 3.1 m3/hr were measured.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.6 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 26-04-2025
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 800 m MD. Excessive cavings (8.4 m3) over the shakers.
     Mud weight raised to 1.18 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 26.0 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 27-04-2025
     Tight hole experienced at 840 m MD in Girujan Clay; 3 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.16 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.9 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 30-04-2025
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.426.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.3 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 04-05-2025
     Differential sticking at 1,930 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 52.4 t.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 15.3 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 48.2 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 09-05-2025
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 2,430 m MD; approximately 7.3 m3 recovered.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.4 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 13-05-2025
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.26.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 23.0 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 14-05-2025
     String became stuck at 3,020 m MD (2,887 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 12.1 t recorded.
     Jarring continued for 3.7 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.0 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  800 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,930 m MD.

[PAGE 4]
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,754 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  840 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  986 m MD.
