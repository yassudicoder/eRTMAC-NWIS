     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.7 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 21-11-2013
     Hole washout logged at 310 m MD in Namsang; caliper 14.3 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.8 hrs.

5.3  Namsang - 22-11-2013
     While drilling Namsang at 390 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 2.5 m3/hr were measured.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.0 hrs.

5.4  Girujan Clay - 23-11-2013
     Tight hole experienced at 920 m MD in Girujan Clay; 7 reaming passes required.
     Reamed and circulated the interval clean before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.3 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 26-11-2013
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.389.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.5 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 28-11-2013
     Lost circulation encountered at 1,960 m MD in Tipam Sandstone. Loss rate recorded as 32.6 m3/hr.
     LCM pill of 82 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 47.7 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 01-12-2013
     Pipe stuck at 2,260 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1511.5 m.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 107 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 59.9 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 05-12-2013
     High torque and drag from 2,670 m MD in Barail Coal Shale; peak torque 25.2 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.9 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 10-12-2013
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.18.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 44.9 hrs.

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 08-12-2013
     Hole instability and sloughing from 3,140 m to 3,152 m MD in Barail Coal Shale.
     Mud weight raised to 1.31 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.5 hrs.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,960 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,260 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,098 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,140 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  920 m MD.
