5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 25-12-2009
     Mud loss of 3.8 m3/hr recorded between 10 m and 60 m MD in Dihing.
     Pumped 127 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.3 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 28-12-2009
     Hole instability and sloughing from 820 m to 864 m MD in Girujan Clay.
     Mud weight raised to 1.23 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.6 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 02-01-2010
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 13-3/8in surface casing at 1,082 m MD.
     Job completed with reduced returns; top of cement confirmed by temperature survey.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 41.8 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 02-01-2010
     Top of cement logged at 545 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 25.8 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 06-01-2010
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,690 m MD. Excessive cavings (8.7 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.31 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 38.9 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 12-01-2010
     Differential sticking at 3,160 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 21.7 t.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 91 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.6 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 13-01-2010
     Well kicked at 3,190 m MD (2,870 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Pit gain 11.1 m3, SIDPP 64.4 ksc. Well shut in.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.33 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 146.9 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,190 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,082 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,082 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around

[PAGE 4]
  2,690 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  820 m MD.
