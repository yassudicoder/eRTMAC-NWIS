5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 24-03-2024
     Seepage losses (2.2 m3/hr) noted at 70 m MD in the Dihing.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 1.9 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.6 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 27-03-2024
     Hole instability and sloughing from 900 m to 923 m MD in Girujan Clay.

[PAGE 3]
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.2 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 05-04-2024
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.383.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.9 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 06-04-2024
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,590 m MD. Excessive cavings (15.2 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.27 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 131.0 hrs.

5.5  Barail Arenaceous - 15-04-2024
     Mud loss of 17.7 m3/hr recorded between 3,160 m and 3,199 m MD in Barail Arenaceous.
     LCM pill of 118 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 36.7 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 24-04-2024
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.326.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.4 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 20-04-2024
     Pipe stuck at 3,380 m MD while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Free point established at 2193.0 m.
     Jarring continued for 37.2 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 45.1 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 22-04-2024
     Influx taken at 3,430 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous. Well shut in, SIDPP 58.5 ksc; killed by drillers method.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.3 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 99.8 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,590 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,430 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - mud loss: losses into the reservoir after raising mud weight for kick margin.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,160 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.

[PAGE 4]
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,380 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,300 m MD.
