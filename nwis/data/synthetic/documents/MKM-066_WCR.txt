5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 03-01-2024
     Hole instability and sloughing from 670 m to 698 m MD in Girujan Clay.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.22 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 28.6 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 06-01-2024
     String became stuck at 1,100 m MD (1,100 m TVD) in Tipam Sandstone. Overpull of 38.6 t recorded.

[PAGE 3]
     Jarring continued for 15.6 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.2 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 08-01-2024
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.326.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.2 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 09-01-2024
     Lost circulation encountered at 1,440 m MD in Tipam Sandstone. Loss rate recorded as 2.4 m3/hr.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.2 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 19-01-2024
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.402.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.3 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 16-01-2024
     Partial mud losses of 30.3 m3/hr observed at 2,940 m MD (2,940 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 9.5 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.7 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 19-01-2024
     Differential sticking at 3,160 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 50.1 t.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 9.9 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 22.2 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  670 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,100 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - mud loss: losses into the reservoir after raising mud weight for kick margin.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,940 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,160 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,126 m MD.
