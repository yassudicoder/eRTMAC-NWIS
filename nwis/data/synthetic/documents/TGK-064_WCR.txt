5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 19-11-2022
     While drilling Namsang at 430 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 3.6 m3/hr were measured.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 4.1 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.0 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 20-11-2022
     Hole pack-off tendency at 570 m MD in the Girujan Clay. Back-reamed 7 times.

[PAGE 3]
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.16 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.1 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 23-11-2022
     Top of cement logged at 113 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.9 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 24-11-2022
     Seepage losses (2.3 m3/hr) noted at 1,380 m MD in the Tipam Sandstone.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 4.5 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.0 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 28-11-2022
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 2,560 m MD; approximately 9.0 m3 recovered.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 38.3 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 03-12-2022
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.336.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.0 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,560 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  570 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,058 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,841 m MD.
- Namsang - mud loss: seepage losses into coarse sand stringers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  430 m MD.
