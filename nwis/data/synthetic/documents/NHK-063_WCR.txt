5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 07-12-2020
     Tight hole experienced at 810 m MD in Girujan Clay; 7 reaming passes required.
     Reamed and circulated the interval clean before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.5 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 18-12-2020
     Top of cement logged at 185 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.1 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 18-12-2020
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.341.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.2 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 13-12-2020
     While drilling Tipam Sandstone at 2,310 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 22.6 m3/hr were measured.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 20.0 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 43.4 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 16-12-2020
     Pipe stuck at 2,480 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1746.3 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 26.2 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 43.0 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 19-12-2020
     Hole instability and sloughing from 2,850 m to 2,854 m MD in Barail Coal Shale.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.3 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.4 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 06-01-2021
     Top of cement logged at 1,435 m against a planned top of 1,050 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.0 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 06-01-2021
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.32.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.3 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 24-12-2020
     Mud loss of 5.6 m3/hr recorded between 3,570 m and 3,623 m MD in Barail Arenaceous.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 4.3 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.9 hrs.

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 29-12-2020
     Differential sticking at 4,030 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 37.6 t.
     Jarring continued for 49.4 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 58.1 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 12-01-2021
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.207.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 41.5 hrs.

5.12  Barail Arenaceous - 12-01-2021
     Primary cement job on the 7in liner failed acceptance; remedial squeeze carried out at 4,074 m MD.
     Squeeze held the pressure test; drilling resumed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.4 hrs.

5.13  Kopili Shale - 05-01-2021
     Torque increased sharply at 4,330 m MD while drilling Kopili Shale, peaking at 35.2 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 56.1 hrs.

5.14  Langpar - 20-01-2021
     Tight hole experienced at 5,074 m MD in Langpar; 2 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.43 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.1 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,310 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,480 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,030 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,074 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
