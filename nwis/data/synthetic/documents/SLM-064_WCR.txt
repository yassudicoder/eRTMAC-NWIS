5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 06-02-2022
     Hole washout logged at 20 m MD in Dihing; caliper 12.6 percent over gauge.

[PAGE 3]
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.7 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 08-02-2022
     Tight hole experienced at 700 m MD in Girujan Clay; 7 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.18 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.8 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 16-02-2022
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.424.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.9 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 12-02-2022
     Seepage losses (16.5 m3/hr) noted at 1,480 m MD in the Tipam Sandstone.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 40.0 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 17-02-2022
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,220 m MD. Excessive cavings (10.6 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.31 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 27.4 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 08-03-2022
     CBL indicated top of cement at 1,166 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate, 393 m below the planned top of 773 m.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.4 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 08-03-2022
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.196.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 55.9 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 25-02-2022
     Well kicked at 2,960 m MD (2,960 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Pit gain 7.3 m3, SIDPP 75.4 ksc. Well shut in.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.36 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 95.7 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 02-03-2022
     Differential sticking at 3,050 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 80.0 t.
     Jarring continued for 76.4 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 140.1 hrs.

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 15-03-2022
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.354.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.

[PAGE 4]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.0 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 10-03-2022
     Seepage losses (4.8 m3/hr) noted at 3,150 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous.
     Pumped 69 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.5 hrs.

5.12  Kopili Shale - 11-03-2022
     Torque increased sharply at 3,230 m MD while drilling Kopili Shale, peaking at 16.9 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.7 hrs.

5.13  Kopili Shale - 13-03-2022
     Well kicked at 3,260 m MD (3,260 m TVD) while drilling Kopili Shale. Pit gain 5.1 m3, SIDPP 27.8 ksc. Well shut in.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.56 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 52.0 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,960 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,050 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,480 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,220 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,525 m MD.
