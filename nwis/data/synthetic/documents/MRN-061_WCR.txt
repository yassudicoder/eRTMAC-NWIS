5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 07-11-2015
     Hole washout logged at 100 m MD in Namsang; caliper 18.0 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.6 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 08-11-2015
     Partial mud losses of 2.3 m3/hr observed at 150 m MD (150 m TVD) while drilling Namsang.

[PAGE 3]
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.4 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 09-11-2015
     Tight hole experienced at 500 m MD in Girujan Clay; 2 reaming passes required.
     Reamed and circulated the interval clean before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.4 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 13-11-2015
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.111.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 39.7 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 15-11-2015
     Differential sticking at 1,990 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 56.9 t.
     Jarring continued for 23.2 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 29.5 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 27-11-2015
     Cement channelling suspected behind the 9-5/8in intermediate at 2,494 m MD; hole was washed out over the interval.
     Additional centralisers run on the following string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 12.5 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 27-11-2015
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.195.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.5 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 20-11-2015
     Differential sticking at 2,640 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 24.1 t.
     Jarring continued for 5.4 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.7 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 22-11-2015
     Gas influx observed at 2,790 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 1275. Pit gain 6.9 m3.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.28 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 149.0 hrs.

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 29-11-2015
     Seepage losses (34.9 m3/hr) noted at 2,850 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous.
     LCM pill of 105 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.8 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[PAGE 4]
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,790 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  794 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,990 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,494 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - mud loss: losses into the reservoir after raising mud weight for kick margin.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,850 m MD.
