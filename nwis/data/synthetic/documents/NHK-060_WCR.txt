5.2  Girujan Clay - 25-11-2023
     Hole instability and sloughing from 790 m to 828 m MD in Girujan Clay.
     Mud weight raised to 1.19 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.3 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 04-12-2023
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.415.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.3 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 28-11-2023
     While drilling Tipam Sandstone at 1,320 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 18.0 m3/hr were measured.
     LCM pill of 56 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 28.1 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 30-11-2023
     Differential sticking at 1,600 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 75.9 t.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 65 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 116.3 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 13-12-2023
     Torque increased sharply at 3,150 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 34.9 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 51.1 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 15-12-2023
     String became stuck at 3,170 m MD (2,702 m TVD) in Barail Coal Shale. Overpull of 35.7 t recorded.
     Jarring continued for 24.3 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 30.6 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 24-12-2023
     Partial returns lost during the 9-5/8in intermediate cement job at 3,426 m MD.
     Job completed with reduced returns; top of cement confirmed by temperature survey.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 47.3 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 24-12-2023
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.209.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 46.0 hrs.

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 24-12-2023
     CBL indicated top of cement at 1,512 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate, 452 m below the planned top of 1,060 m.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.0 hrs.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,600 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,426 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,860 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,320 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,150 m MD.
