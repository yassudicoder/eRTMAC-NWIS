5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 30-06-2020
     Lost circulation encountered at 370 m MD in Namsang. Loss rate recorded as 4.4 m3/hr.
     Pumped 83 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.9 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 01-07-2020
     Hole washout logged at 410 m MD in Namsang; caliper 41.0 percent over gauge.

[PAGE 3]
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.6 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 03-07-2020
     Bit balling suspected at 780 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 31.5 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.6 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 07-07-2020
     Bond log showed channelling behind the 13-3/8in surface, casing poorly centralised.
     Squeeze performed across the channelled interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.3 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 07-07-2020
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.17.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 45.9 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 05-07-2020
     Differential sticking at 950 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 90.9 t.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 84 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 145.4 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 12-07-2020
     Mud loss of 3.0 m3/hr recorded between 1,110 m and 1,140 m MD in Tipam Sandstone.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 4.7 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.2 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 24-07-2020
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.424.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.6 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 25-07-2020
     Torque increased sharply at 2,680 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 17.6 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.7 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  950 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around

[PAGE 4]
  915 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: washed-out hole left the casing poorly centralised, so the slurry channelled up one side of the annulus.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  915 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,627 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,680 m MD.
