     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 24.9 hrs.

     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.362.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.9 hrs.
