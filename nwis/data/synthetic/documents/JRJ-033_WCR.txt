5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 14-09-2010
     Hole washout logged at 600 m MD in Namsang; caliper 36.5 percent over gauge.

[PAGE 3]
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.4 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 15-09-2010
     Bit balling suspected at 980 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 19.7 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.3 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 16-09-2010
     Tight hole experienced at 1,150 m MD in Girujan Clay; 13 reaming passes required.
     Reamed and circulated the interval clean before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.5 hrs.

5.4  Girujan Clay - 17-09-2010
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 1,180 m MD. Excessive cavings (3.3 m3) over the shakers.
     Mud weight raised to 1.23 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.1 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 20-09-2010
     Partial mud losses of 4.7 m3/hr observed at 1,580 m MD (1,580 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     Pumped 90 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.5 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 11-10-2010
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.397.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.6 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 01-10-2010
     Well kicked at 3,160 m MD (3,160 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Pit gain 10.6 m3, SIDPP 47.4 ksc. Well shut in.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.32 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 149.0 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 16-10-2010
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.243.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 44.5 hrs.

5.9  Kopili Shale - 12-10-2010
     Torque increased sharply at 3,810 m MD while drilling Kopili Shale, peaking at 39.0 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 42.2 hrs.

5.10  Kopili Shale - 14-10-2010
     Well kicked at 3,840 m MD (3,840 m TVD) while drilling Kopili Shale. Pit gain 9.0 m3, SIDPP 65.7 ksc. Well shut in.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.58 sg.

     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 98.3 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,160 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,840 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,150 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,587 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - high torque and drag: narrow mud-weight window and cuttings loading.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,810 m MD.
