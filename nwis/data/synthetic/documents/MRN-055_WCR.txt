5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 21-09-2017
     Hole washout logged at 20 m MD in Dihing; caliper 23.7 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.4 hrs.

5.2  Dihing - 21-09-2017
     Mud loss of 2.6 m3/hr recorded between 20 m and 78 m MD in Dihing.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 3.0 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.0 hrs.

5.3  Namsang - 21-09-2017
     Hole washout logged at 50 m MD in Namsang; caliper 73.9 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.3 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 26-09-2017
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.154.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.3 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 26-09-2017
     Lost circulation encountered at 1,120 m MD in Tipam Sandstone. Loss rate recorded as 9.1 m3/hr.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.9 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 28-09-2017
     Differential sticking at 1,330 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 14.9 t.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 66 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 12.6 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 07-10-2017
     Sustained casing pressure observed after the 9-5/8in intermediate cement job; annular gas migration suspected at 2,551 m MD.
     Annulus bled down and monitored; gas-tight slurry used on subsequent strings.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 30.2 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 07-10-2017
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.242.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 33.0 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 11-10-2017
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.257.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.5 hrs.

5.10  Kopili Shale - 12-10-2017
     Hole instability and sloughing from 3,470 m to 3,517 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.5 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  828 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: gas percolated through the setting cement, producing sustained casing pressure.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,551 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,551 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,179 m MD.
- Namsang - hole washout: poorly consolidated sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  50 m MD.
