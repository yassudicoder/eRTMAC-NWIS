------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 04-06-2024
     Hole washout logged at 80 m MD in Dihing; caliper 12.3 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.0 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 06-06-2024
     Tight hole experienced at 890 m MD in Girujan Clay; 2 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.2 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.2 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 07-06-2024
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 1,030 m MD. Excessive cavings (2.2 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.21 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.7 hrs.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  890 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,121 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,500 m MD.
- Dihing - hole washout: unconsolidated gravel eroded by high flow rate.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  80 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,030 m MD.
