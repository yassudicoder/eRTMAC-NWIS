5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Tipam Sandstone - 27-01-2022
     While drilling Tipam Sandstone at 1,960 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 25.5 m3/hr were measured.
     LCM pill of 140 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 44.3 hrs.

5.2  Barail Coal Shale - 03-02-2022
     High torque and drag from 2,850 m MD in Barail Coal Shale; peak torque 18.7 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.2 hrs.

5.3  Barail Coal Shale - 13-02-2022
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.378.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.9 hrs.

5.4  Barail Arenaceous - 09-02-2022
     Gas influx observed at 3,400 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 675. Pit gain 4.3 m3.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.32 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 28.6 hrs.

5.5  Kopili Shale - 16-02-2022
     Hole instability and sloughing from 3,910 m to 3,950 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.52 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 53.6 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,960 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,400 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - wellbore instability: over-pressured shale sloughing.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,910 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,850 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,097 m MD.
