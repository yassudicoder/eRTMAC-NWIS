5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 19-11-2020
     Bit balling suspected at 840 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 17.3 ksc.
     Raised flow rate and added detergent to the system.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.4 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 26-11-2020
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.358.

[PAGE 3]
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.4 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 25-11-2020
     Partial mud losses of 5.8 m3/hr observed at 1,790 m MD (1,790 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     Pumped 117 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.0 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 03-12-2020
     Torque increased sharply at 2,780 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 15.0 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.1 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 09-12-2020
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.397.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.4 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 08-12-2020
     Well kicked at 3,130 m MD (3,130 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Pit gain 4.6 m3, SIDPP 38.1 ksc. Well shut in.
     Weighted up to 1.37 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 26.9 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 09-12-2020
     Differential sticking at 3,150 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 42.6 t.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 132 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 35.2 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,130 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,150 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,217 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,790 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,780 m MD.
