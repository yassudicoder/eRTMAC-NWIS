5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 23-04-2015
     Bit balling suspected at 540 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 30.8 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.2 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 24-04-2015
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Girujan Clay at 880 m MD; approximately 1.8 m3 recovered.
     Mud weight raised to 1.24 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.3 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 30-04-2015
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.27.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 49.8 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 30-04-2015
     Pipe stuck at 1,930 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1561.2 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 17.9 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.6 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 03-05-2015
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 2,290 m MD; approximately 13.4 m3 recovered.
     Mud weight raised to 1.29 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 103.3 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 15-05-2015
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.202.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 51.4 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 22-05-2015
     Gas migration through the setting cement behind the 7in liner at 3,341 m MD.
     Annulus bled down and monitored; gas-tight slurry used on subsequent strings.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 45.3 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 22-05-2015
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.184.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 29.8 hrs.

5.9  Kopili Shale - 18-05-2015
     High torque and drag from 3,510 m MD in Kopili Shale; peak torque 14.0 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.5 hrs.

[PAGE 4]

5.10  Kopili Shale - 25-05-2015
     Well kicked at 4,140 m MD (3,364 m TVD) while drilling Kopili Shale. Pit gain 4.7 m3, SIDPP 25.6 ksc. Well shut in.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.58 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 59.1 hrs.

5.11  Kopili Shale - 29-05-2015
     Hole instability and sloughing from 4,310 m to 4,357 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Mud weight raised to 1.52 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.5 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,290 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  924 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,636 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: gas percolated through the setting cement, producing sustained casing pressure.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,341 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,341 m MD.
