5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 14-06-2024
     Hole washout logged at 80 m MD in Dihing; caliper 29.8 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.9 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 17-06-2024
     Hole instability and sloughing from 990 m to 1,035 m MD in Girujan Clay.

[PAGE 3]
     Mud weight raised to 1.22 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.3 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 24-06-2024
     Bond log showed channelling behind the 13-3/8in surface, casing poorly centralised.
     Additional centralisers run on the following string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.5 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 24-06-2024
     Primary cement job on the 13-3/8in surface failed acceptance; remedial squeeze carried out at 1,125 m MD.
     Second squeeze required before an acceptable test was obtained.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 40.4 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 24-06-2024
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.24.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 49.8 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 20-06-2024
     String became stuck at 1,150 m MD (1,150 m TVD) in Tipam Sandstone. Overpull of 82.8 t recorded.
     Jarring continued for 71.4 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 122.5 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 25-06-2024
     Partial mud losses of 11.2 m3/hr observed at 1,290 m MD (1,290 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     LCM pill of 39 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.7 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 30-06-2024
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,390 m MD. Excessive cavings (18.3 m3) over the shakers.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 124.5 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 05-07-2024
     Differential sticking at 2,400 m MD across the Barail Coal Shale; maximum overpull 34.6 t.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 16.0 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 38.7 hrs.

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 07-07-2024
     Torque increased sharply at 2,510 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 28.6 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.5 hrs.

5.11  Barail Coal Shale - 12-07-2024
     Partial returns lost during the 9-5/8in intermediate cement job at 2,800 m MD.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.

[PAGE 4]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 22.9 hrs.

5.12  Barail Coal Shale - 12-07-2024
     Top of cement logged at 1,745 m against a planned top of 842 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.3 hrs.

5.13  Barail Coal Shale - 12-07-2024
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.172.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 25.2 hrs.

5.14  Barail Arenaceous - 12-07-2024
     Seepage losses (9.5 m3/hr) noted at 3,020 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous.
     LCM pill of 156 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.1 hrs.

5.15  Barail Arenaceous - 13-07-2024
     Gas influx observed at 3,120 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 1394. Pit gain 9.7 m3.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.32 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 41.9 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,150 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,390 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,120 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,125 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,125 m MD.
