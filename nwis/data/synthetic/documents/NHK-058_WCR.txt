5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 14-07-2016
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Girujan Clay at 680 m MD; approximately 3.5 m3 recovered.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.22 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.9 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 17-07-2016
     Pipe stuck at 1,180 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1062.5 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 24.4 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 30.3 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 19-07-2016
     While drilling Tipam Sandstone at 1,200 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 8.3 m3/hr were measured.
     Pumped 65 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.0 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 21-07-2016
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 13-3/8in surface casing at 1,240 m MD.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 42.2 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 21-07-2016
     CBL indicated top of cement at 427 m behind the 13-3/8in surface, 427 m below the planned top of 0 m.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 26.6 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 21-07-2016
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.445.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.0 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,180 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,240 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,240 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  680 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,200 m MD.
