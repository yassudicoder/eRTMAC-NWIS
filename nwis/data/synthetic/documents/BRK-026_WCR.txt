5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 06-07-2009
     Tight hole experienced at 690 m MD in Girujan Clay; 7 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.22 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.3 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 06-07-2009
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Girujan Clay at 740 m MD; approximately 9.5 m3 recovered.

[PAGE 3]
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 53.3 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 09-07-2009
     Bit balling suspected at 890 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 30.1 ksc.
     Raised flow rate and added detergent to the system.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.2 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 10-07-2009
     Top of cement logged at 173 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.9 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 10-07-2009
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.391.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.7 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 14-07-2009
     Mud loss of 4.2 m3/hr recorded between 2,100 m and 2,113 m MD in Tipam Sandstone.
     LCM pill of 132 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.1 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 15-07-2009
     Hole instability and sloughing from 2,330 m to 2,354 m MD in Barail Coal Shale.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 29.5 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 18-07-2009
     High torque and drag from 2,780 m MD in Barail Coal Shale; peak torque 40.6 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 39.9 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 22-07-2009
     Partial returns lost during the 9-5/8in intermediate cement job at 2,800 m MD.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 53.7 hrs.

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 22-07-2009
     CBL indicated top of cement at 1,820 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate, 912 m below the planned top of 908 m.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 29.5 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 22-07-2009
     Partial mud losses of 4.6 m3/hr observed at 3,020 m MD (3,020 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous.
     Pumped 63 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  740 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,330 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,780 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,800 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,800 m MD.
