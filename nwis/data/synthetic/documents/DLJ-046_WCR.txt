5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 26-03-2024
     Hole washout logged at 40 m MD in Dihing; caliper 16.1 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.2 hrs.

5.2  Barail Coal Shale - 09-04-2024
     Pipe stuck at 2,730 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale. Free point established at 1740.4 m.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 105 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 52.7 hrs.

5.3  Barail Coal Shale - 13-04-2024
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 3,060 m MD; approximately 9.7 m3 recovered.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.24 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 56.0 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 19-04-2024
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.357.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.1 hrs.

5.5  Barail Arenaceous - 17-04-2024
     Gas influx observed at 3,310 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 2280. Pit gain 6.4 m3.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.36 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 73.0 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 17-04-2024
     Lost circulation encountered at 3,310 m MD in Barail Arenaceous. Loss rate recorded as 2.5 m3/hr.
     Pumped 141 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.2 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,310 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - stuck pipe: pack-off on coal cavings.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,730 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,060 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,158 m MD.
- Dihing - hole washout: unconsolidated gravel eroded by high flow rate.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  40 m MD.
