5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Tipam Sandstone - 01-05-2011
     Seepage losses (10.9 m3/hr) noted at 1,110 m MD in the Tipam Sandstone.
     LCM pill of 50 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 12.3 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 06-05-2011
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.322.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.9 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 03-05-2011
     Pipe stuck at 1,410 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 768.9 m.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 123 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 44.7 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 10-05-2011
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,770 m MD. Excessive cavings (9.0 m3) over the shakers.
     Mud weight raised to 1.27 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 38.1 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 13-05-2011
     Pipe stuck at 3,080 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale. Free point established at 1873.1 m.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 112 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.5 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 14-05-2011
     Torque increased sharply at 3,230 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 16.9 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 11.2 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 23-05-2011
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.344.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 11.5 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 18-05-2011
     Influx taken at 3,650 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous. Well shut in, SIDPP 63.4 ksc; killed by drillers method.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.29 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 144.1 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 26-05-2011
     Differential sticking at 3,890 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 27.8 t.
     Jarring continued for 12.9 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.6 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.

[PAGE 4]
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,650 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,410 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,770 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,110 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,154 m MD.
