5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 20-10-2016
     Tight hole experienced at 670 m MD in Girujan Clay; 4 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.24 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.9 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 22-10-2016
     Seepage losses (3.4 m3/hr) noted at 1,190 m MD in the Tipam Sandstone.

[PAGE 3]
     LCM pill of 77 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.1 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 25-10-2016
     Top of cement logged at 219 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.1 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 25-10-2016
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.38.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.2 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 27-10-2016
     Differential sticking at 2,230 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 18.6 t.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 9.2 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.1 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 05-11-2016
     Top of cement logged at 1,275 m against a planned top of 1,067 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.6 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 05-11-2016
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.345.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.1 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 01-11-2016
     Gas influx observed at 3,110 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 664. Pit gain 3.6 m3.
     Weighted up to 1.28 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 22.6 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 03-11-2016
     Seepage losses (35.1 m3/hr) noted at 3,200 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous.
     LCM pill of 148 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 48.9 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,110 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - mud loss: losses into the reservoir after raising mud weight for kick margin.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around

  3,200 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  670 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,214 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,214 m MD.
