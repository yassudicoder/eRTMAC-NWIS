5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 09-06-2012
     Lost circulation encountered at 460 m MD in Namsang. Loss rate recorded as 4.0 m3/hr.
     Pumped 68 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.1 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 10-06-2012
     Hole pack-off tendency at 760 m MD in the Girujan Clay. Back-reamed 3 times.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.22 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.9 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 14-06-2012
     Pipe stuck at 1,490 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1209.6 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 34.7 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 47.2 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[PAGE 3]
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,490 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  760 m MD.
- Namsang - mud loss: seepage losses into coarse sand stringers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  460 m MD.
