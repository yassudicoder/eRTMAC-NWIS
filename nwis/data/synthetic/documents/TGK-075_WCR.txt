5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 13-06-2011
     Hole washout logged at 30 m MD in Dihing; caliper 77.1 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 12.2 hrs.


[PAGE 3]
5.2  Dihing - 13-06-2011
     While drilling Dihing at 30 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 7.8 m3/hr were measured.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 8.6 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.1 hrs.

5.3  Namsang - 15-06-2011
     Mud loss of 10.9 m3/hr recorded between 120 m and 138 m MD in Namsang.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 12.4 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.4 hrs.

5.4  Girujan Clay - 17-06-2011
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 550 m MD. Excessive cavings (6.0 m3) over the shakers.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.6 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 20-06-2011
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.427.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.4 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 21-06-2011
     Lost circulation encountered at 1,410 m MD in Tipam Sandstone. Loss rate recorded as 6.4 m3/hr.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.3 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 25-06-2011
     High torque and drag from 2,330 m MD in Barail Coal Shale; peak torque 33.2 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 54.5 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 28-06-2011
     Hole instability and sloughing from 2,440 m to 2,499 m MD in Barail Coal Shale.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.26 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.3 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 04-07-2011
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 9-5/8in intermediate casing at 2,613 m MD.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.3 hrs.

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 04-07-2011
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.195.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.2 hrs.

5.11  Barail Coal Shale - 04-07-2011

[PAGE 4]
     Remedial cement squeeze performed behind the 9-5/8in intermediate at 2,613 m MD before drilling ahead.
     Second squeeze required before an acceptable test was obtained.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 38.5 hrs.

5.12  Barail Coal Shale - 04-07-2011
     CBL indicated top of cement at 1,584 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate, 920 m below the planned top of 664 m.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 53.9 hrs.

5.13  Barail Arenaceous - 01-07-2011
     Well kicked at 2,780 m MD (2,780 m TVD) while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Pit gain 2.9 m3, SIDPP 28.4 ksc. Well shut in.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.3 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 39.3 hrs.

5.14  Kopili Shale - 06-07-2011
     Hole instability and sloughing from 3,190 m to 3,199 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.52 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 45.4 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,330 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,440 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,613 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,613 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,613 m MD.
