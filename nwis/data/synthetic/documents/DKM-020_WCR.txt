5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 01-01-2017
     Hole washout logged at 30 m MD in Namsang; caliper 25.8 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.4 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 03-01-2017
     Bit and BHA balling up in the Girujan Clay at 390 m MD, SPP increase of 10.8 ksc.
     Raised flow rate and added detergent to the system.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.0 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 06-01-2017
     Hole washout logged at 1,360 m MD in Tipam Sandstone; caliper 18.0 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.8 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 19-01-2017
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.325.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.2 hrs.

5.5  Barail Arenaceous - 12-01-2017
     Gas influx observed at 2,570 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 986. Pit gain 9.7 m3.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.32 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 59.3 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 25-01-2017
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.07.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 38.8 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 25-01-2017
     Primary cement job on the 7in liner failed acceptance; remedial squeeze carried out at 2,882 m MD.
     Second squeeze required before an acceptable test was obtained.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 45.3 hrs.

5.8  Kopili Shale - 19-01-2017
     Torque increased sharply at 3,210 m MD while drilling Kopili Shale, peaking at 22.9 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.4 hrs.

5.9  Kopili Shale - 24-01-2017
     Hole instability and sloughing from 3,700 m to 3,734 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 102.5 hrs.

[PAGE 4]

5.10  Kopili Shale - 31-01-2017
     Influx taken at 4,040 m MD in the Kopili Shale. Well shut in, SIDPP 70.6 ksc; killed by drillers method.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.58 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 53.8 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,570 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - wellbore instability: over-pressured shale sloughing.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,700 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,040 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,882 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,882 m MD.
