5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 18-01-2020
     While drilling Dihing at 160 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 3.0 m3/hr were measured.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.2 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 24-01-2020
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.327.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 11.6 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 24-01-2020
     Pipe stuck at 1,460 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1172.0 m.
     Jarring continued for 6.2 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.9 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[PAGE 3]
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,277 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,460 m MD.
- Dihing - mud loss: seepage into permeable gravel beds.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  160 m MD.
