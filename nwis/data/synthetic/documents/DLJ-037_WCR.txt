5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 03-09-2020
     Hole washout logged at 50 m MD in Dihing; caliper 25.0 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.2 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 07-09-2020
     Tight hole experienced at 890 m MD in Girujan Clay; 7 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.17 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.0 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 07-09-2020
     Bit and BHA balling up in the Girujan Clay at 900 m MD, SPP increase of 38.2 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 12.7 hrs.

[PAGE 3]

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 10-09-2020
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.119.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 37.7 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 10-09-2020
     Bond log showed channelling behind the 13-3/8in surface, casing poorly centralised.
     Additional centralisers run on the following string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.3 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 10-09-2020
     Remedial cement squeeze performed behind the 13-3/8in surface at 1,124 m MD before drilling ahead.
     Second squeeze required before an acceptable test was obtained.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 46.1 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,124 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,124 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  890 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - bit balling: sticky clay packing the bit and BHA.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  900 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: washed-out hole left the casing poorly centralised, so the slurry channelled up one side of the annulus.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,124 m MD.
