5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 09-03-2018
     Hole washout logged at 210 m MD in Namsang; caliper 47.1 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.2 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 12-03-2018
     Tight hole experienced at 920 m MD in Girujan Clay; 6 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.15 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.9 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 21-03-2018
     Pipe stuck at 2,480 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 2294.0 m.
     Jarring continued for 12.4 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.0 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 10-04-2018
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.443.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.8 hrs.

5.5  Barail Arenaceous - 29-03-2018
     String became stuck at 3,290 m MD (2,899 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 23.8 t recorded.
     Jarring continued for 3.9 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.9 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 18-04-2018
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.293.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 52.4 hrs.

5.7  Kopili Shale - 06-04-2018
     High torque and drag from 4,060 m MD in Kopili Shale; peak torque 22.5 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.6 hrs.

5.8  Kopili Shale - 07-04-2018
     Wellbore instability in Kopili Shale from 4,130 m MD. Excessive cavings (10.7 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.6 sg.

[PAGE 4]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 52.1 hrs.

5.9  Sylhet Limestone - 15-04-2018
     Total loss of returns at 4,680 m MD (4,072 m TVD) in Sylhet Limestone. No returns to surface.
     LCM pill of 153 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 84.6 hrs.

5.10  Basement - 03-05-2018
     ROP dropped to 1.38 m/hr at 5,440 m MD in Basement.
     Bit pulled and replaced; drilling parameters optimised.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.3 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sylhet Limestone - mud loss: total losses into fractured and karstified limestone.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,680 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,910 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - wellbore instability: over-pressured shale sloughing.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,130 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - tight hole: plastic clay creeping and closing the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  920 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,480 m MD.
