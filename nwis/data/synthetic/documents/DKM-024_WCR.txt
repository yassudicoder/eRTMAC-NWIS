5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 20-04-2016
     While drilling Dihing at 20 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 3.5 m3/hr were measured.
     Pumped 147 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.4 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 23-04-2016
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.308.

[PAGE 3]
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.0 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 23-04-2016
     Primary cement job on the 13-3/8in surface failed acceptance; remedial squeeze carried out at 648 m MD.
     Squeeze held the pressure test; drilling resumed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 56.2 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 27-04-2016
     Lost circulation encountered at 1,520 m MD in Tipam Sandstone. Loss rate recorded as 8.1 m3/hr.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 7.6 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.6 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 28-04-2016
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 1,760 m MD. Excessive cavings (8.3 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.27 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.5 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 01-05-2016
     Torque increased sharply at 2,080 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 23.9 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.6 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 03-05-2016
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.269.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.1 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  648 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,760 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,242 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  648 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,520 m MD.
