5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Namsang - 29-03-2020
     Hole washout logged at 60 m MD in Namsang; caliper 57.8 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.9 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 30-03-2020
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 230 m MD. Excessive cavings (11.5 m3) over the shakers.

[PAGE 3]
     Mud weight raised to 1.19 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 47.7 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 01-04-2020
     Hole pack-off tendency at 270 m MD in the Girujan Clay. Back-reamed 6 times.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.21 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.7 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 04-04-2020
     Bond log showed channelling behind the 13-3/8in surface, casing poorly centralised.
     Squeeze performed across the channelled interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.1 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 04-04-2020
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.121.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.9 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 06-04-2020
     Differential sticking at 1,070 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 47.0 t.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 40.5 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 46.0 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 19-04-2020
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.442.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 11.1 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 19-04-2020
     Seepage losses (11.4 m3/hr) noted at 2,480 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.9 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 21-04-2020
     Pipe stuck at 2,640 m MD while drilling Barail Arenaceous. Free point established at 2377.0 m.
     Jarring continued for 24.4 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 30.4 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  230 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around

[PAGE 4]
  699 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,070 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,640 m MD.
- Namsang - hole washout: poorly consolidated sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  60 m MD.
