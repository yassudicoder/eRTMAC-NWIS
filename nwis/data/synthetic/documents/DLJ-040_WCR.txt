5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 20-01-2022
     Hole washout logged at 10 m MD in Dihing; caliper 33.0 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.6 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 21-01-2022
     Hole washout logged at 410 m MD in Namsang; caliper 34.8 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.9 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 24-01-2022
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Girujan Clay at 980 m MD; approximately 8.0 m3 recovered.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.23 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 59.1 hrs.

5.4  Girujan Clay - 27-01-2022
     Bit and BHA balling up in the Girujan Clay at 1,100 m MD, SPP increase of 22.3 ksc.
     Pulled off bottom, circulated and jetted the bit clean.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 5.8 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 06-02-2022
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.415.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.9 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 30-01-2022
     Pipe stuck at 1,540 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1264.4 m.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 126 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 56.1 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 08-02-2022
     Torque increased sharply at 2,860 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 17.9 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 8.2 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 09-02-2022
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 3,010 m MD. Excessive cavings (9.6 m3) over the shakers.
     Mud weight raised to 1.32 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 57.3 hrs.


[PAGE 4]
5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 12-02-2022
     Pipe stuck at 3,110 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale. Free point established at 1563.5 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 10.3 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 23.9 hrs.

5.10  Barail Arenaceous - 08-03-2022
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.283.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 25.9 hrs.

5.11  Barail Arenaceous - 16-02-2022
     Influx taken at 3,350 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous. Well shut in, SIDPP 67.2 ksc; killed by drillers method.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.36 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 115.1 hrs.

5.12  Barail Arenaceous - 26-02-2022
     String became stuck at 3,780 m MD (3,375 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 91.3 t recorded.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 107 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 69.5 hrs.

5.13  Barail Arenaceous - 18-03-2022
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.121.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 35.1 hrs.

5.14  Kopili Shale - 05-03-2022
     Hole instability and sloughing from 4,040 m to 4,088 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Mud weight raised to 1.51 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.4 hrs.

5.15  Kopili Shale - 08-03-2022
     Gas influx observed at 4,240 m MD in Kopili Shale; gas units rose to 2989. Pit gain 7.5 m3.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.56 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 106.6 hrs.

5.16  Sylhet Limestone - 16-03-2022
     Complete loss of circulation at 4,410 m MD on entering Sylhet Limestone.
     Pumped 71 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 147.1 hrs.

5.17  Langpar - 30-03-2022
     Tight hole experienced at 4,890 m MD in Langpar; 5 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.4 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.6 hrs.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,350 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,780 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,240 m MD.
- Sylhet Limestone - mud loss: total losses into fractured and karstified limestone.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,410 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  980 m MD.
