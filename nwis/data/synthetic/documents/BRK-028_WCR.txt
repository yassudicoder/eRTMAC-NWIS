5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 21-12-2024
     Bit balling suspected at 590 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 28.5 ksc.
     Raised flow rate and added detergent to the system.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 4.2 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 26-12-2024
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.445.

[PAGE 3]
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.0 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 25-12-2024
     Pipe stuck at 1,280 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1011.7 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 20.7 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 34.1 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 30-12-2024
     Mud loss of 16.9 m3/hr recorded between 2,140 m and 2,167 m MD in Tipam Sandstone.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 11.5 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.8 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 02-01-2025
     Torque increased sharply at 2,440 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 33.8 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 50.7 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 07-01-2025
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.199.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 31.6 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,280 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,140 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,440 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,683 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,020 m MD.
