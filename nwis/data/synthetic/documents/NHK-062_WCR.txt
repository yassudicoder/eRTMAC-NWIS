5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 19-10-2012
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 960 m MD. Excessive cavings (8.4 m3) over the shakers.

[PAGE 3]
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 50.9 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 28-10-2012
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.412.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.4 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 24-10-2012
     Hole washout logged at 1,660 m MD in Tipam Sandstone; caliper 12.4 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.7 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 26-10-2012
     Pipe stuck at 2,010 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 1807.4 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 8.3 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.3 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 13-11-2012
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.303.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.0 hrs.

5.6  Barail Coal Shale - 03-11-2012
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 3,080 m MD; approximately 11.2 m3 recovered.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.29 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 53.1 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 08-11-2012
     Differential sticking at 3,430 m MD across the Barail Arenaceous; maximum overpull 56.4 t.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 20.8 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 42.7 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 18-11-2012
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.194.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 23.8 hrs.

5.9  Kopili Shale - 13-11-2012
     Well kicked at 3,800 m MD (3,800 m TVD) while drilling Kopili Shale. Pit gain 9.4 m3, SIDPP 58.3 ksc. Well shut in.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.56 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 85.5 hrs.

5.10  Kopili Shale - 17-11-2012
     Hole instability and sloughing from 3,810 m to 3,850 m MD in Kopili Shale.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.57 sg.

     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 31.0 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,800 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  960 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,010 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,080 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,430 m MD.
