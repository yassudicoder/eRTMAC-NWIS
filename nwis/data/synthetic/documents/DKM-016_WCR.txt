5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Tipam Sandstone - 29-05-2021
     Hole washout logged at 610 m MD in Tipam Sandstone; caliper 31.2 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.2 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 01-06-2021
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 13-3/8in surface casing at 629 m MD.
     Job completed with reduced returns; top of cement confirmed by temperature survey.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.4 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 01-06-2021
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.349.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.2 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 01-06-2021
     Primary cement job on the 13-3/8in surface failed acceptance; remedial squeeze carried out at 629 m MD.
     Second squeeze required before an acceptable test was obtained.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 35.3 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 01-06-2021
     Top of cement logged at 175 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 58.8 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 30-05-2021
     Partial mud losses of 12.4 m3/hr observed at 660 m MD (660 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 8.3 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.6 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 06-06-2021
     Differential sticking at 1,740 m MD across the Tipam Sandstone; maximum overpull 86.0 t.
     Jarring continued for 29.6 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 46.1 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 12-06-2021
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 2,180 m MD; approximately 11.8 m3 recovered.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 57.5 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 15-06-2021
     High torque and drag from 2,320 m MD in Barail Coal Shale; peak torque 25.6 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.3 hrs.

5.10  Barail Coal Shale - 18-06-2021
     Top of cement logged at 811 m against a planned top of 505 m behind the 9-5/8in intermediate.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.4 hrs.

     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.241.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,740 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  629 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  629 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  629 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,180 m MD.
