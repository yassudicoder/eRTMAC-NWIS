5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Tipam Sandstone - 10-04-2009
     Partial mud losses of 19.8 m3/hr observed at 1,320 m MD (1,320 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 35.6 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 59.3 hrs.

5.2  Barail Coal Shale - 19-04-2009
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,910 m MD. Excessive cavings (3.2 m3) over the shakers.

[PAGE 3]
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.24 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.6 hrs.

5.3  Barail Coal Shale - 24-04-2009
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.276.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 32.7 hrs.

5.4  Barail Arenaceous - 22-04-2009
     While drilling Barail Arenaceous at 3,240 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 14.6 m3/hr were measured.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.5 hrs.

5.5  Barail Arenaceous - 25-04-2009
     String became stuck at 3,420 m MD (3,420 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 39.1 t recorded.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 38.5 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 48.1 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,320 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,977 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,420 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,910 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - mud loss: losses into the reservoir after raising mud weight for kick margin.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,240 m MD.
