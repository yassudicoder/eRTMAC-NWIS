5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Girujan Clay - 02-01-2012
     Wellbore instability in Girujan Clay from 400 m MD. Excessive cavings (1.7 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.22 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.2 hrs.

5.2  Barail Coal Shale - 16-01-2012
     Wellbore instability in Barail Coal Shale from 2,730 m MD. Excessive cavings (15.4 m3) over the shakers.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.3 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 147.8 hrs.

5.3  Barail Coal Shale - 02-02-2012
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.275.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 37.9 hrs.

5.4  Barail Arenaceous - 24-01-2012
     Seepage losses (5.2 m3/hr) noted at 3,030 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.0 hrs.

5.5  Barail Arenaceous - 26-01-2012
     Influx taken at 3,110 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous. Well shut in, SIDPP 51.6 ksc; killed by drillers method.
     Weighted up to 1.32 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 141.5 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 02-02-2012
     String became stuck at 3,300 m MD (3,043 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 93.1 t recorded.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 29.9 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 51.0 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 10-02-2012
     Partial returns lost during the 7in liner cement job at 3,441 m MD.
     Cut slurry density and continued; losses were cured before the plug bumped.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 37.9 hrs.

5.8  Barail Arenaceous - 10-02-2012
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.254.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 20.3 hrs.

5.9  Barail Arenaceous - 10-02-2012
     CBL indicated top of cement at 2,864 m behind the 7in liner, 274 m below the planned top of 2,590 m.
     Accepted after review; previous shoe covered by the subsequent string.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 35.7 hrs.

5.10  Langpar - 18-02-2012
     Hole pack-off tendency at 4,289 m MD in the Langpar. Back-reamed 5 times.

     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.2 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,730 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,110 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,300 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,814 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,441 m MD.
