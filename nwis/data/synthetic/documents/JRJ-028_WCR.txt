5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 12-03-2009
     Hole washout logged at 90 m MD in Dihing; caliper 30.4 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.0 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 14-03-2009
     Hole washout logged at 350 m MD in Namsang; caliper 69.4 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 16.2 hrs.

5.3  Girujan Clay - 16-03-2009
     Hole instability and sloughing from 810 m to 842 m MD in Girujan Clay.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 25.0 hrs.

5.4  Girujan Clay - 17-03-2009
     Bit balling suspected at 860 m MD in Girujan Clay; standpipe pressure rose by 19.0 ksc.
     Raised flow rate and added detergent to the system.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.7 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 24-03-2009
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.272.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 42.2 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 20-03-2009
     Hole washout logged at 1,440 m MD in Tipam Sandstone; caliper 74.7 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.2 hrs.

5.7  Barail Coal Shale - 24-03-2009
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Barail Coal Shale at 2,500 m MD; approximately 18.9 m3 recovered.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 39.4 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 27-03-2009
     String became stuck at 2,590 m MD (2,590 m TVD) in Barail Coal Shale. Overpull of 43.2 t recorded.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 21.7 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 57.8 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 30-03-2009
     Well kicked at 2,700 m MD (2,700 m TVD) while drilling Barail Coal Shale. Pit gain 2.5 m3, SIDPP 26.3 ksc. Well shut in.

6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Coal Shale - wellbore instability: coal seams caving and producing large cavings at the shakers.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,500 m MD.
- Langpar - mud loss: losses into fractured carbonate.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  4,290 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  810 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,204 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - stuck pipe: pack-off on coal cavings.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,590 m MD.
