5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Tipam Sandstone - 16-09-2019
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.396.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.0 hrs.

5.2  Tipam Sandstone - 15-09-2019
     String became stuck at 1,440 m MD (1,440 m TVD) in Tipam Sandstone. Overpull of 33.4 t recorded.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 29.7 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 39.0 hrs.

5.3  Barail Coal Shale - 30-09-2019
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.369.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.6 hrs.

5.4  Barail Coal Shale - 26-09-2019
     Torque increased sharply at 2,810 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 41.5 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 21.3 hrs.

5.5  Barail Arenaceous - 28-09-2019
     While drilling Barail Arenaceous at 3,010 m MD, returns reduced and losses of 11.5 m3/hr were measured.
     Cement plug placed across the loss zone and drilled out after 5.6 hrs WOC.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 17.8 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 01-10-2019
     String became stuck at 3,210 m MD (2,874 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 46.0 t recorded.
     Jarring continued for 48.0 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 56.3 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,440 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - high torque and drag: cavings packing the annulus and loading the string.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,810 m MD.
- Barail Arenaceous - stuck pipe: differential sticking across the depleted pay.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,210 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  864 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,730 m MD.
