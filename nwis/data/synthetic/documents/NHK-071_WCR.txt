5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 25-09-2014
     Seepage losses (3.3 m3/hr) noted at 50 m MD in the Dihing.

[PAGE 3]
     Pumped 79 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 3.8 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 28-09-2014
     Hole instability and sloughing from 760 m to 771 m MD in Girujan Clay.
     Mud weight raised to 1.15 sg and inhibition increased; hole condition improved.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.3 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 06-10-2014
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.387.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 15.6 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 05-10-2014
     Partial mud losses of 9.1 m3/hr observed at 1,820 m MD (1,820 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     LCM pill of 58 bbl (medium nut plug and mica) pumped; losses reduced.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.3 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 22-10-2014
     Cement bond log across the 9-5/8in intermediate showed poor bonding, bond index 0.242.
     Accepted after pressure testing the shoe; monitored for annular pressure.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 54.3 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 17-10-2014
     Seepage losses (10.2 m3/hr) noted at 3,430 m MD in the Barail Arenaceous.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.2 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 27-10-2014
     Cement bond log across the 7in liner showed poor bonding, bond index 0.421.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 13.0 hrs.

5.8  Kopili Shale - 22-10-2014
     Gas influx observed at 3,910 m MD in Kopili Shale; gas units rose to 2363. Pit gain 7.0 m3.
     Well shut in and killed by drillers method. Mud weight raised to 1.58 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 120.0 hrs.

5.9  Kopili Shale - 27-10-2014
     Cavings observed at shakers while drilling Kopili Shale at 3,930 m MD; approximately 9.6 m3 recovered.
     Increased KCl concentration and raised mud weight to 1.56 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 27.8 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[PAGE 4]
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,910 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,130 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - wellbore instability: over-pressured shale sloughing.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,930 m MD.
- Girujan Clay - wellbore instability: reactive clay hydrating and sloughing into the hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  760 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: bond log showed poor to fair bonding across the interval, risking annular communication.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,351 m MD.
