5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 28-04-2012
     Hole washout logged at 10 m MD in Dihing; caliper 51.0 percent over gauge.
     Noted for cement volume calculation; no remedial action taken.

[PAGE 3]
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 9.1 hrs.

5.2  Namsang - 29-04-2012
     Lost circulation encountered at 220 m MD in Namsang. Loss rate recorded as 2.1 m3/hr.
     Pumped 24 bbl of coarse LCM slurry and cut mud weight by 0.03 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.5 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 01-05-2012
     Partial mud losses of 30.7 m3/hr observed at 740 m MD (740 m TVD) while drilling Tipam Sandstone.
     Reduced flow rate and continued drilling blind with water.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 37.0 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 04-05-2012
     Cement slurry lost to the formation while cementing 13-3/8in surface casing at 753 m MD.
     Job completed with reduced returns; top of cement confirmed by temperature survey.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 112.4 hrs.

5.5  Tipam Sandstone - 04-05-2012
     CBL/VDL run on the 13-3/8in surface indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.391.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 14.8 hrs.

5.6  Tipam Sandstone - 04-05-2012
     Top of cement logged at 290 m against a planned top of 0 m behind the 13-3/8in surface.
     Top-up job performed through the annulus to raise the cement top.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 35.3 hrs.

5.7  Tipam Sandstone - 09-05-2012
     Pipe stuck at 1,740 m MD while drilling Tipam Sandstone. Free point established at 979.6 m.
     Back-off performed and fishing assembly run; fish recovered after 33.2 hrs.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 46.7 hrs.

5.8  Barail Coal Shale - 16-05-2012
     String became stuck at 2,380 m MD (2,221 m TVD) in Barail Coal Shale. Overpull of 51.9 t recorded.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 123 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 31.8 hrs.

5.9  Barail Coal Shale - 20-05-2012
     CBL/VDL run on the 9-5/8in intermediate indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.305.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.6 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement slurry lost into a permeable or fractured interval, leaving the top of cement below the planned depth.

[PAGE 4]
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  753 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - mud loss: high-permeability sandstone taking whole mud.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  740 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - cementing problem: cement column fell short of the planned top, leaving the previous shoe uncovered.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  753 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,740 m MD.
- Barail Coal Shale - stuck pipe: pack-off on coal cavings.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,380 m MD.
