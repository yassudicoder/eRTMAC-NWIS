5. DRILLING PROBLEMS AND REMEDIAL MEASURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Dihing - 31-07-2017
     Hole washout logged at 20 m MD in Dihing; caliper 42.6 percent over gauge.
     Reduced flow rate through the interval.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 2.2 hrs.

5.2  Girujan Clay - 03-08-2017
     Tight hole experienced at 690 m MD in Girujan Clay; 6 reaming passes required.
     Worked the string over the tight interval and raised mud weight to 1.17 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 6.8 hrs.

5.3  Tipam Sandstone - 07-08-2017
     Cement bond log across the 13-3/8in surface showed poor bonding, bond index 0.344.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 7.0 hrs.

5.4  Tipam Sandstone - 09-08-2017
     String became stuck at 1,860 m MD (1,860 m TVD) in Tipam Sandstone. Overpull of 52.7 t recorded.
     Jarring continued for 22.2 hrs before the string came free.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 43.2 hrs.

5.5  Barail Coal Shale - 12-08-2017
     Torque increased sharply at 2,150 m MD while drilling Barail Coal Shale, peaking at 24.4 kNm.
     Added lubricant to the mud system and reduced RPM.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.0 hrs.

5.6  Barail Arenaceous - 18-08-2017
     String became stuck at 2,660 m MD (2,660 m TVD) in Barail Arenaceous. Overpull of 22.9 t recorded.
     Worked and jarred the string; spotted 54 bbl of pipe-release pill. String freed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.2 hrs.

5.7  Barail Arenaceous - 19-08-2017
     Gas influx observed at 2,770 m MD in Barail Arenaceous; gas units rose to 747. Pit gain 6.4 m3.
     Circulated out the influx through the choke; kill mud weight 1.35 sg.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 106.1 hrs.

5.8  Kopili Shale - 29-08-2017
     CBL/VDL run on the 7in liner indicated poor to fair bond, bond index 0.308.
     Remedial squeeze carried out and the bond log re-run; bond improved to fair.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 10.5 hrs.

5.9  Kopili Shale - 29-08-2017
     Remedial cement squeeze performed behind the 7in liner at 3,197 m MD before drilling ahead.
     Squeeze held the pressure test; drilling resumed.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 28.6 hrs.

[PAGE 4]

5.10  Kopili Shale - 27-08-2017
     High torque and drag from 3,200 m MD in Kopili Shale; peak torque 28.1 kNm.
     Circulated sweeps to clean the hole; torque returned to normal.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 19.2 hrs.

5.11  Kopili Shale - 28-08-2017
     Wellbore instability in Kopili Shale from 3,210 m MD. Excessive cavings (10.2 m3) over the shakers.
     Circulated hi-vis sweeps and back-reamed the interval before continuing.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 18.3 hrs.

5.12  Kopili Shale - 30-08-2017
     Well kicked at 3,280 m MD (3,145 m TVD) while drilling Kopili Shale. Pit gain 2.9 m3, SIDPP 41.2 ksc. Well shut in.
     Weighted up to 1.53 sg and circulated bottoms up twice before resuming.
     Non-productive time attributable to this event: 47.7 hrs.


6. LESSONS LEARNT AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE WELLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Barail Arenaceous - kick: gas influx from the Barail pay sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  2,770 m MD.
- Tipam Sandstone - stuck pipe: differential sticking against depleted permeable sand.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  1,860 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - cementing problem: the primary cement job failed acceptance and a remedial squeeze was required before drilling ahead.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,197 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - wellbore instability: over-pressured shale sloughing.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,210 m MD.
- Kopili Shale - kick: overpressured shale entering an under-balanced hole.
  Future wells on this structure should plan for this around
  3,280 m MD.
